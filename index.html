Proximity classification

As you adjust the eye size, the tool checks whether the ratio is near a notable value: a power of φ or Φ, a Fibonacci ratio, a harmonic fraction, or a geometric or transcendental constant. The match is labelled by how close it is.

Exact (within ~1 billionth)
Indistinguishable from the known value at double precision. Use the equation box (Phi/6, phi^3) for exact values.
Near-Exact (within ±0.1%)
Well within any perceptible visual difference; a practical stand-in.
Adjacent (within ±0.5%)
Clearly related; a careful measurement would reveal the gap.
Near (within ±2%)
A visible family resemblance.
Vicinal (within ±5%)
In the neighbourhood. Borrowed from crystallography, where it names a face just off a principal plane.

Powers of Φ and φ are checked up to the 9th power, with divisors to 30. Fibonacci ratios, harmonic fractions, √2, √3, √5 forms, and π and e ratios are catalogued too.
